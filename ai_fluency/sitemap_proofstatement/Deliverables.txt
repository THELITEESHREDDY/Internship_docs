5. Call to Action (CTA)

Purpose
The CTA is the actual interface element that converts the visitor into your desired action.
Your primary CTA should be:
Contact me about an engineering role →
You can make it slightly more contextual:
Talk to me about an engineering role →
or:
Build AI systems with me →
But I'd keep the first one because it directly matches your defined action.
Where it appears
You should have the same primary CTA at strategic points:
Hero
  ↓
[See the proof]

Proof
  ↓
[Contact me about an engineering role]

Final section
  ↓
[Contact me about an engineering role]

The hero can use “See the proof” as a navigation CTA because the visitor hasn't earned the conversion yet.
The actual conversion CTA remains:
Contact me about an engineering role.
Deliverables
  One primary CTA wording
  One destination
  Contact mechanism works
  CTA appears after sufficient proof
  CTA is visually dominant over secondary links
  No competing “primary” CTAs
